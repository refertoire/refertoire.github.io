The Problem & The Fix

Singers lose precious rehearsal time hunting through messaging threads and dealing with unorganized files. Refertoire solves choir score management once and for all.

Status Quo

Disorganized Repertoire Tracking

How choral groups lose rehearsal time and struggle to keep pieces organized

  • Scattered Score Files PDFs spread across messaging apps, email threads, and download folders with no single catalog.
  • Difficult Song Classification Singers cannot quickly sort pieces by voicing (SATB), liturgical seasons, mass parts, or composer.
  • Disorganized Rehearsal Flow Rehearsals stall while choristers hunt for page cuts, lost copies, or realize they have an outdated revision.
  • Sanctuary Signal Dropouts Scores fail to open when rehearsing inside stone churches or venue basements with zero reception.

Built for Choirs & Ensembles

Five essential pillars designed specifically for choral rehearsals, liturgical seasons, concert performances, and local file management.

1-Step Code Sync

Connect your entire choir in seconds with a single 8-character ensemble access code. Prompts singers to pick their preferred local folder, then saves, references, and caches all sheet music PDFs directly into that chosen directory for 100% offline access. No passwords or account creation needed for singers.

Study Mode with Built-in PDF Viewer & Page Flip

Built-in high-DPI PDF viewer engineered for rehearsals and study. Features tactile single-page flip turns, horizontal swipe, or continuous vertical scroll, with Bluetooth foot-pedal turning and glare-free stage dark mode.

Sorting by Song Classification

Instantly sort and filter your repertoire library by choral voicings (SATB, SSAA, TTBB, SAB), liturgical seasons (Advent, Lent, Easter, Ordinary Time), musical genres, key signatures, and composer surnames.

Scheduling & Grouping Pieces by Performance

Group pieces into dedicated concert programs, liturgical services, and rehearsal setlists. Directors arrange the performance order once; every singer's digital binder re-sequences automatically.

Mobile & Web Local Folders

User-Selected Folder Storage (Pick, Save & Reference Anywhere)

Refertoire asks singers to pick a folder where the repertoire will be saved into. The app references and manages your sheet music PDFs directly from that chosen directory (on Android storage, or your PC and Mac hard drive, Dropbox, or iCloud), syncing files automatically whenever the director updates scores.
