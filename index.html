Built for Choirs & Ensembles

Five essential pillars designed specifically for choral rehearsals, liturgical seasons, concert performances, and local file management.

1-Step Code Sync

Connect your entire choir in seconds with a single 8-character ensemble access code. Instantly syncs the full repertoire manifest and caches all sheet music PDFs directly to local device storage—no passwords or account creation needed for singers.

Study Mode with Built-in PDF Viewer & Page Flip

Built-in high-DPI PDF viewer engineered for rehearsals and study. Features tactile single-page flip turns, horizontal swipe, or continuous vertical scroll, with Bluetooth foot-pedal turning and glare-free stage dark mode.

Sorting by Song Classification

Instantly sort and filter your repertoire library by choral voicings (SATB, SSAA, TTBB, SAB), liturgical seasons (Advent, Lent, Easter, Ordinary Time), musical genres, key signatures, and composer surnames.

Scheduling & Grouping Pieces by Performance

Group pieces into dedicated concert programs, liturgical services, and rehearsal setlists. Directors arrange the performance order once; every singer's digital binder re-sequences automatically.

Desktop Disk & iOS Files

Local Directory Sync Agent (Web for PC & iOS)

For PC and Mac users, the web application functions as an automated file manager sync agent, mirroring the choir's sheet music and folder hierarchy directly into your chosen local drive (Dropbox, iCloud, or hard disk). For iPad and iPhone users who cannot sideload APKs, export structured folder archives straight into the iOS Files app or dispatch scores into forScore and PiaScore in one click.
